Questions

Can I print these worksheets?

Yes! Tap “Download PDF” on any worksheet. Answer keys are in the “For teachers & parents” section at the bottom of each worksheet.

Is there sound?

Yes – a happy chime and a cheer for every right answer. Use the Sound and Voice buttons at the top of a worksheet to switch them off.

Do children have to guess?

No – every question asks the child to work it out and write the answer. Pictures help them see the idea first.

Do children need to sign in?

No. Just open a worksheet and play.

Where should we start?

Follow the 🎯 worksheets in order – they follow the learning order, from sharing to long division.
